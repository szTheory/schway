# Research and provenance ledger

## Disposition definitions

- **Admitted:** supported by a primary source authoritative for the mechanism.
- **Corrected:** an authoritative primary source contradicted the prior claim.
- **Unresolved:** evidence is insufficient, indirect, conflicting, or does not
  establish the broader design conclusion.
- **Decision:** the author explicitly selected or delegated a scoped choice;
  implementation hypotheses beneath it can remain unresolved.
- **Project evidence:** a reproducible local experiment and its exact bounded
  result; it establishes only the scope named by the experiment.

Primary sources establish what a system does. Recommendations derived from them
remain synthesis.

## Source-quality notes

- Official language manuals establish semantics but may naturally emphasize
  benefits; comparative recommendations remain synthesis.
- Recent AI-code papers establish results for particular models, datasets, and
  languages. They do not establish universal language-design laws.
- Current version/status claims are temporal and must be rechecked before a
  roadmap commits to external runtimes or toolchains.
