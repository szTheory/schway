<objective>
Close EVD-02: every requirement and success-criterion row carries a grade from a
closed vocabulary, and a row below the satisfying bar cannot be recorded as
satisfied. The grade is **declared by the author and mechanically capped** — a
test derives a ceiling from the row's own evidence cell and fails when the
declared grade exceeds it.

Purpose, and the objection the plan must answer first: a reviewer will say this
crosses the deliberate line the debt-register law draws, where register *honesty*
stays a human reading. It does not. A grade is an honesty property in general,
but EVD-02 needs it only as a **shape property at the bar**: the two grades that
*satisfy* a requirement are fully mechanically decidable from a resolving test
name, while the three that refuse are refusals — and nobody games a control by
under-claiming. The cap mechanizes only the pass side and leaves the refusal side
declarative.

The rejected alternative matters too: validating only that the cell is a member
of the closed set would be a short clone of the existing severity vocabulary that
still lets a checker write a wiring-level verdict with a spellchecker attached.
**No seeded fault can make a string-membership check go red, so it cannot carry a
non-inertness proof** — and a control that cannot be proven non-inert is exactly
what this phase exists to retire.

Output: a derivation ladder, a cap, a total-order assertion, a file-scoped dated
exemption map, fourteen migrated verification maps, and a re-pinned lint frontier.
</objective>
