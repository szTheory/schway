### Origin refusal diagnostic
**Source:** `originPeerRefusalDiagnostic(problem, program, tree)` session.go:849.

### Do-not-widen boundary
**Source:** `publishedOriginProblemFile` rationale session.go:1227-1239.

**Apply to:** the guide-contract execution; never use raw `os/exec`.
