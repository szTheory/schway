<objective>
Prove the whole Phase 4 spine end-to-end on one thin slice: a Lang program that declares a foreign C symbol, calls it fallibly through `try`, and either returns on the ok edge or terminates with a typed failure on the err edge — checked, independently validated, interpreted, lowered to C17, linked against the byte-frozen foreign translation unit, and run at -O0 and -O3 with the interpreter agreeing.

Purpose: SEM-03 and FFI-01 both need a call construct and a failure control shape that the language does not have today. Nothing else in this phase is demonstrable until one fallible foreign call exists at every layer, and `native.validateExecution`'s current hard-reject of any outcome other than `"returned"` blocks every later native task until it is widened.
Output: four additive `OperationKind` values behind a single `core.AllOperationKinds()` registry, the exhaustive six-site dispatch control, the `foreign C { }` admission surface with its two mandatory policy declarations, the frozen foreign translation unit, and one fixture proven through the shipped binary at both optimization levels.
</objective>
