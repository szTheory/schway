<objective>
Close verification gap 1 by making the release-order rederivation's independence
FALSIFIABLE, and by adding the structural peer check the gap asks for in the one

`checkReleaseOrder` skipped any terminal block whose incoming-edge count was not
exactly 1. The skip itself was removed by commit `16fb0c9` (the WR/CR fix pass) —

revert-and-fail demonstrations, and two Mutation-Kill Register rows.

refuses a terminal block with zero incoming edges via
`core.release_order_indeterminate` and rederives against EVERY incoming edge.
What is missing is (a) any test that exercises a multi-incoming-edge terminal
block at all, (b) the `blocksAndEdges` structural peer check
