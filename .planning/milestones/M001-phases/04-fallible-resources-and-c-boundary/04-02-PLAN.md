<objective>
Give the phase its resource lifecycle: three fallible acquisitions, partial initialization, and a release sequence that is materialized once by `check`, rederived independently by `corevalidate`, executed identically by both engines, and falsified by two mutations that attack two different artifacts.

Purpose: RES-01 and ROADMAP success criterion 1 require that partially initialized noncopyable resources release exactly once in reverse completed-acquisition order on return and on typed failure. CONTEXT.md's sharpest research finding is that a two-acquisition fixture cannot distinguish release order from release set, so this plan ships three.
Output: the `OpRelease` operation kind, the `discard ... because` consumer, reverse-order materialization, an independent backward rederivation, three fixtures covering success and both partial-failure stages, and the transposition and omission controls.
</objective>
