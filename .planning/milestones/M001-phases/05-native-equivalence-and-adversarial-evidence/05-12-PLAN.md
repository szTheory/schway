<objective>
Define the `lang.mismatch/0` document, emit it on any injected mismatch, and
prove the reducer can go red in all three of the ways a reducer can be vacuous.

Purpose: INT-02 requires that a mismatch report the smallest known source/core
case, causal facts, and an addressable event trace. The document's primary
consumer is an AI repair agent, which must be able to propose a fix from this
document alone without opening the full execution log. And a reducer has three
distinct ways to be vacuous — no-op, predicate too loose, non-determinism — so
D-05-27 requires three distinct mutation-kills, not one.

Output: the schema type with a golden round-trip, the session wiring that emits it
on divergence, and the three required kills.
</objective>
