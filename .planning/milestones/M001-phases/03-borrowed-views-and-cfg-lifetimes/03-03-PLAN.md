<objective>
Replace the checker's transitive per-binding loan-set propagation with backward worklist dataflow over the CFG, producing edge-specific loan endpoints and honest counted work.

Purpose: ROADMAP criterion 2 requires a branch-specific last use to end a loan on the correct CFG edge, with the omission detectable. D-05 makes the Theta(N-squared) checker cost this phase's problem, and D-02-03's correction records that the checker counts nothing while the validator counts honestly, which is precisely why `recomputed_work` understates.
Output: a fixpoint liveness pass, per-edge loan endpoints on the core artifact, a linear counted-work series, and the accept/reject fixture pair that falsifies uniform join placement.
</objective>
