Attribute cost to pipeline stages without a tracing runtime, and record the peak-RSS gap as
a deliberate decision rather than an oversight.

that a flagged wall-clock observation must cite the responsible stage's work-count delta
before it can become a gate candidate. PROJECT.md forbids a mandatory global tracing
runtime, so this is explicit start/stop pairs at the small number of fixed, sequential
boundaries the pipeline already crosses (D-06-21).
Output: `stage_breakdown` populated, gated, identity-excluded, and the `peak_rss_status`
rationale recorded in code.
</objective>
