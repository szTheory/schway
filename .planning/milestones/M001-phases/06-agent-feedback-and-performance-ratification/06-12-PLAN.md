<objective>
Build the five defect injectors and the held-out corpus they operate on, each with a
fail-closed marker guard.

Purpose: DX-04's "introduce, locate, and repair representative match, move, borrow, cleanup,
and stale-evidence defects". This plan does the INTRODUCE and LOCATE halves plus the third
anti-theater guard; 06-13 does REPAIR and 06-14 does the two remaining guards.

The third guard is first-class work here, not test polish: if an injector's target
disappears — because a lowering change removed the marker, say — an exercise with no guard
reports green while testing nothing. That is the exact failure fingerprint behind this
project's three standing process rules.
Output: `testdata/phase6/`, five injectors, five marker guards.
</objective>
