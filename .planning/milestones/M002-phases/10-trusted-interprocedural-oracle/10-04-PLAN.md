<objective>
Give SEM-08 its documented fixed ceiling and its named refusal, and prove with a
subprocess probe that the language-level bound and the host stack limit are two
structurally unrelated limits rather than one limit wearing two names.

Purpose: a bound that cannot fire on any program the compiler will actually
admit is decorative. `MaxCallDepth` must sit BELOW the real structural ceiling so
that it DOES fire on a legitimately generated program — the exact inverse of how
`pathoracle.MaxPaths` is set. That inversion is the crux of this plan and must be
written into the source.

Output: `MaxCallDepth` with its inverted rationale, the depth-exceeded refusal as
comparable `Outcome` data, a 129-function chain driven through the REAL pipeline,
and the Pitfall-4 subprocess probe with its threshold fixed in source before the
first green run.
</objective>
