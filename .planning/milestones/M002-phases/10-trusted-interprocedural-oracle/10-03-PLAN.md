  <decision>
Handle `core.OpCall` in `pathoracle` by COMPOSING callee paths — recursing into
the callee via pathoracle's own enumerator and splicing its concrete paths into
the caller's — rather than by adding a contract hop on a callee summary fact.
  </decision>
  <reversibility>one-way</reversibility>
  <rationale>
The composed enumeration becomes the substrate that QLT-04's declared-depth
corpus (plan 10-07) and criterion 4's differential (plan 10-08) are both built
on. Retreating to a contract hop afterwards invalidates the depth declaration
and the differential's independence claim TOGETHER — it is not a local refactor.

The decision is already LOCKED in 10-CONTEXT.md as D-10-09 and is not being
reopened. This checkpoint exists because the reversibility rating is `one-way`:
confirm-and-proceed, so the irreversibility is acknowledged when it is incurred.

The property TRU-03 actually rests on (D-10-11) is NOT "touches zero other
functions" — impossible for any correct interprocedural analysis. It is "never
converges, never closes a relation, only enumerates and replays concrete
paths." Composition preserves that under call-crossing; a contract hop does not,
because it collapses a callee's per-path distinctions into one summary fact
BEFORE replay — precisely the join/reduction step the package doc says this
package never performs.
  </rationale>
