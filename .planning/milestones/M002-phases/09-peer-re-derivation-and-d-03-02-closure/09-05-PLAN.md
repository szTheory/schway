<objective>
Prove that ownership transfer at a call site has exactly one meaning: derived
independently by `check` and `corevalidate` from the callee's declared parameter
convention, with call-site override of that convention non-expressible in source
and fail-closed in core.

Purpose: this is the one criterion of the five that is proven mostly by ABSENCE,
and absence is the easiest thing to assert loosely. "Not expressible in source"
and "not expressible in core" are two different claims about two different trust
levels, and `corevalidate`'s whole role is validating a core it did not produce —
so they must be proven separately or the weaker one silently stands in for the
stronger.

Output: a grammar-level structural-absence test, a core-level field-absence test
plus confirmation of the existing closed-set decode control, and a cross-peer
agreement assertion on the per-call-site classification.
</objective>

## Planner findings the executor must not re-derive

- **Verified: `core.LinearOperation` carries no convention-override field.** Its
  fields are `ID`, `PointID`, `Kind`, `SourceID`, `TargetID`, `LoanID`, `TypeID`,
  `OkEdgeID`, `ErrEdgeID`, `ErrTargetID`, `ReleasesOperationID`, `Allocator`, and
  Phase 07's `CalleeID` (`core/core.go:657-700`). There is no undecoded slot for a
  call-site override to hide in.
- **Verified: the grammar cannot express it.** `callArguments()`
  (`syntax/parser.go:501-515`) parses each argument with
  `p.identifier("syntax.expected_call_argument")` and returns `[]string`. A
  `take` / `borrow` / `borrow mut` prefix is admissible ONLY on a binding's RHS
  (`parser.go:402-409`), never inside a call's argument list, and
  `ast.RHS.Arguments` is a `[]string` — structurally incapable of carrying a
  per-argument mode even if the parser did accept one.
- **Verified: the core-level fail-closed control already exists.**
  `core/core.go:462` and `:470` reject any `ParameterContract.Mode` or
  `ReturnContract.Mode` outside `{"owned", "shared", "exclusive"}` at decode
  time with `core.interface_invalid_mode`. This is the control to CONFIRM and
  EXTEND coverage of, not a place to add a seam.
- **Verified: both producers hardcode `Mode: "owned"` with the reason stated.**
  `corevalidate.derivePeerSignature` (`corevalidate.go:2011-2015`) and
  `originvalidate.go:811` each carry an explicit "D-07-01: today's grammar has
  exactly one parameter form" comment.
- **The distinction from D-07-47 / D-08-15 is the point.** Those minted seams
  because a real, reachable-looking code path existed and was proven closed. Here
  the field does not exist, so a seam would be dead weight filling a shape that
  is not there.
