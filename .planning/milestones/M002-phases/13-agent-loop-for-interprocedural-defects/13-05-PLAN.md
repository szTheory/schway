<objective>
Complete Set A: attach the remaining two repairs — `wrap_call_in_try` and
`use_matching_argument` — so `lang-repair` reaches three genuinely distinct
interprocedural defect families, and pay the last of D-13-09a's diagnostic-ID churn
deliberately.

Purpose: DX-07's "at least three new interprocedural defect classes". Class 2 is
interprocedural by construction — only the callee's declaration makes the call
fallible — and carries zero semantic-drift risk. Class 3 is the one class where a
clean-checking-but-semantically-different program is reachable, and D-13-10's
uniqueness gate IS the entire safety argument: it is rustc's "there is a value of
this type in scope" `MaybeIncorrect` downgrade converted into a PRECONDITION rather
than an applicability downgrade, consistent with `NormalizeApplicability`'s refusal
to ever default toward driver-eligible.

Output: two more repair kinds, the uniqueness gate with its positive
`unrepairable` test, and the final two re-pinned diagnostic IDs.

**This plan runs after the held-out corpus is sealed (plan 13-04).** That ordering
is deliberate: the split is drawn before the repairs are written, per D-13-27 and
the APR overfitting lesson. Tune only against `derivation_*` fixtures.

**Planner assumption, surfaced not dropped:** the edge-coverage probe classified
DX-07 as `unclassified` — no generic edge category resolves it. It remains
UNRESOLVED. The planner's explicit assumption is that DX-07's edge behavior is
pinned by D-13-10's zero / exactly-one / two-or-more partition on the uniqueness
gate and by D-13-29's exact-outcome-string discipline, and by nothing else. If a
fourth partition appears in practice (for example a match whose place is in scope
but not initialized), escalate rather than widen the gate.

two or more, call `ErrorWithRepairs` with an empty repair slice.

clean-checking-but-semantically-different program is reachable, and the uniqueness
gate is the entire safety argument.
