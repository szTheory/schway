<objective>
Build QLT-03's call-graph-shape reachability register: a mechanically enumerated
axis product recording which cross-function shapes the generator actually
reaches, and naming — with one admissible proof mechanism each — the shapes it
provably does not.

Purpose: safety is a property of the DECLARED production space, not of observed
output. A register whose cells can be absent, or whose negative claims are prose,
records nothing. This one cannot have an absent cell, rejects any mechanism
outside a closed set, treats a thinly-reached cell as a gap, and is proven able to
fail.

Output: `qlt03_shape_register.go`, its committed JSON artifact, the audit, and the
drift protections that keep it from going vacuous.
</objective>
