<objective>
Make `reduce` a first-class subject of this phase rather than a downstream
consumer: accept a multi-function seed, add exactly two whole-program HDD moves
that run at the coarse level, derive the attempt bound instead of hard-coding it,
and ship an enumerated register of the shapes the reducer provably declines.

Purpose: criterion 4's "HDD reducer output on multi-function programs is
re-verified" cannot be met by widening `cgen` alone — `reduce.Reduce` hard-errors
on a multi-function seed and `ProjectSource` returns an unsupported-projection
string. This plan removes both walls while preserving byte-identical output for
every existing single-function reduction.

Output: `Seed`, the two new moves, the derived bound, `RefusedShapes()`, and the
structural guard that keeps `reduce` out of `callgraph`'s consumer set.
</objective>
