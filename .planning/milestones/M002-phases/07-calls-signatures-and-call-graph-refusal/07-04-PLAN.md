phase-07-scoped fixture lists, make them assert **recognition rather than
execution**, and mutation-kill each of them **in this plan**.

sweeps: Phase 07 must add its own. And per **D-07-39** a control that asserts only
"the switch had an arm" would go green on a stub, so both controls must state, in
code and in doc comment, that they prove recognition and never execution.

`linearProbeInput` arm; `session_phase7.go` with its own lane and
`Phase7RequiredControls()`; `scripts/verify-phase7.sh` with its set-equality peer
test; the recognized-not-executed assertions for `interp` and `cgen`; and a seeded
mutation for each control, in this plan (D-07-41).
