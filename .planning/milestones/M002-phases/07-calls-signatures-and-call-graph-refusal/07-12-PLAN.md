and `.Fails` document themselves as *"closure-derived worst-case foreign reach"*
and *"empty means infallible"*, but both are computed with a strictly local

`core.go:406-407` documents as *"a LEGAL value meaning **no foreign reach**"*. A
body-blind consumer — precisely the consumer `lang.interface/1` exists for —
acts on a false claim.

Phase 07 is the phase that introduced those edges and that DID chain
`ClosureDigest` across them (07-08, `BuildInterface`'s second pass over
`chainOrder`), so the closure machinery is present and was simply not applied
to these two fields. And, as with CR-01 before it, the producer and its peer
share the blind spot verbatim: `corevalidate.derivePeerSignature`
(corevalidate.go:1920-1928) reproduces the producer's local read exactly, so the
bilateral check certifies the false claim rather than catching it. This is
undeclared in PHASE-07-DEBT.md, so it is an omission, not a cut.

Output: an explicit worst-case lattice join over both fields in the producer's
existing acyclic second pass, computed before `ClosureDigest`; an independently
implemented join in `corevalidate`'s own `chainPeerClosureDigests` over its own
postorder; `testdata/phase07/call_fallible_foreign_reach.lang` as the standing
witness; the IN-01 index-coupling fix in the same pass; two new controls, each
mutation-killed on both sides and wired into `Phase7RequiredControls()` and the

granularity limits.

<assumption_delta_decision>
`gsd_run query assumption-delta scan "07" --json` returned `detected: true`
(signals: `pluralization`). The honest delta in this plan is a
`derived → chosen` anchor move, recorded rather than dismissed.

- **Noun that is now primary:** a function's **published foreign reach and
  fallibility** are properties of its CALL CLOSURE, not of its own declaration.
  Previously the declaration WAS the authority and the closure was not consulted.
- **Decision: `promote`.** The closure-derived value is promoted to the
  published value; the function-local `ForeignContract` read is demoted from
  "the answer" to "this function's own contribution to a join". This is a
  promote, not an add-alongside: no second, function-local field is retained
  beside the joined one, because two fields would let a consumer read the
  narrower one and re-create the false claim.
- **Consequence recorded:** the published `Foreign`/`Fails` of every caller of a
  foreign-reaching callee CHANGES, and so does that caller's `ClosureDigest`
  (the join runs before the digest, by design). Task 1 must therefore enumerate
  every place a `ClosureDigest` or a summary is committed to disk and prove none
  is a golden being silently regenerated.
- **What would force further change:** a third policy value beyond
  `forbidden`/`permitted`, or a second allocator vocabulary. The join is defined
  field-by-field over today's vocabulary and would need re-specifying — recorded
  as debt in Task 3, not left implicit.
</assumption_delta_decision>

<threat_model>
Security enforcement is active at **ASVS L1**, blocking threshold **high**. The
security-relevant defect class here is **a false claim in a distributable
artifact**: `lang.interface/1` summaries are consumed body-blind, so a published
field that contradicts its documented semantics is acted on as truth by a
consumer that has no way to check it.

## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| `core.Program` -> published `lang.interface/1` summary | The artifact a body-blind consumer trusts absolutely. A false `foreign`/`fails` here is a false claim that cannot be locally falsified. |
| producer summary -> `corevalidate.derivePeerSignature` peer | The independent second derivation. CR-03 proves it currently reproduces the producer's blind spot verbatim. |
| callee's published signature -> caller's joined signature | The new closure channel. It must read signatures only — never a callee body — or SEM-05 breaks by construction. |
| joined facts -> `ClosureDigest` -> cache/staleness detection | The digest must cover the joined facts, or a callee's foreign-reach change would not invalidate its callers. |
