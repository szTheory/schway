<objective>
Stage 1a. Give `core.OpCall` the callee identity it never had, register it in
`AllOperationKinds()` — deliberately breaking all six exhaustive switches at once
— and land the producer path plus honest arms at every consumer.

Purpose: this is the review's second-highest finding. `CalleeID` appears **zero**
times across the five superseded plans and `07-CONTEXT.md`, while `07-04` twice
said *"an `OpCall` whose callee **resolves** to no declared function"* —
presupposing a mechanism that was never specified. `core.LinearOperation`
(`core.go:261-300`) has `SourceID`, `TargetID`, `LoanID`, `TypeID`, the three
foreign-edge fields, `ReleasesOperationID`, `Allocator`, and `Reason` — and no
callee identity. `OpForeignCall`'s precedent does not generalize: it hangs symbol
identity off `Function.ForeignContract`, viable only because a function has at
most one foreign contract, whereas a function may call many callees. Without
`CalleeID`, `callgraph` has no edges and every plan after this one has no input.

Output: `core.LinearOperation.CalleeID`; `ast.RHS` kind `"call"`; `core.OpCall`
registered; the `check` producer path; explicit "recognized, unsupported" arms in
`interp` and `cgen`; the relocated foreign bare-call refusal with its code
preserved; and the two Stage 1 fixtures.
</objective>
