<objective>
Deliver the MOD-06 runnable gain. A second application, `adler_low`, imports the same `adler32` library (D-03) through the D-16 trailing-call shape and prints a different pinned result for the same file: 920 against 300286872 for Wikipedia. Purpose: SC3 says "imported, not copied". Only a mutation that flips both applications at once, together with a scan that finds one loop, demonstrates it.

Output: `adler_low.schway`, its public-route test, the single-copy and dual-flip test, and the register rows.
</objective>
