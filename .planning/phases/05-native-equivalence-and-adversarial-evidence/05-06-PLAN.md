<objective>
Add the `-O3 -flto` tier that makes the differential non-vacuous, and turn the
comparison axes and their exclusion list from prose into asserted, fail-closed
code.

Purpose: D-05-24 is the sharpest finding in the whole research set. Without
`-flto`, a call to a symbol defined in a separate translation unit is opaque at
the call site — Clang cannot inline it, cannot prove it pure, cannot eliminate
effects across it. Every current corpus program's only observable effect crosses
exactly that boundary, so the existing differential is green *by construction*,
not by evidence, which is precisely the failure shape D-09/D-10 exist to prevent.
Separately, SC1's wording silently omits exit status/signal and reject-program
diagnostic equivalence; D-05-20 adds both, and D-05-21 makes the exclusion list
fail-closed so a pointer cannot leak into a compared field.

Output: an LTO build configuration with a proof it is not inert, a Phase 5
comparator with five compared axes, and a reflection-driven field-routing test
that fails the build on any unrouted field.
</objective>
