## Material second-pass findings

1. **CR-04 diagnosis corrected.** Fresh checked/corevalidated Phase 24 modeled failures ended at `OpFail`, after `foreign.failed`. The simple Phase24-derived error-block `OpReturn` mutation was refused by `corevalidate`. The original synthetic probe lacks type/place/CFG facts and cannot establish admitted reachability. Preserve that history and target the real peer rejection instead; do not claim all raw-core returns are impossible.
2. **False proof boundary rejected.** Adding event kind/suffix cases does not prove foreign outcomes or branch choices. The event stream has no failure payload/branch witness. The selected contract stays causal, with independently pinned model values; no new wire payload or general evaluator.
3. **Reason-byte obligation retained.** Raw U+0001 and NUL source bytes are admitted. A fresh actual native application probe reproduced corrupted/truncated stderr for 01 41, 00 41 and 00 5A. Counted field emission is required to uphold the exact-reason promise; fixed octal escaping alone is insufficient. The intended exit/prefix/newline contract and ordinary ASCII tuples remain fixed.
4. **Compatibility is explicit.** Semantic repair wins for affected C outputs, before emitter edits. Old goldens stay immutable; active amendments require exact rows/routes and a reviewed delta. The checked-in sum_to_n baseline is a candidate, not an exhaustive affected-row assertion.

## Research and evidence

The durable `discussion-evidence/README.md` owns probe commands, outputs and selected bindings. Diagnostic PASS means the observed mismatch/refusal was reached, not that these bugs are repaired. Existing audit receipts are historical; advisors' source inspection is not newly passing runtime evidence.

Primary comparisons informed the synthesis: [Go panic propagation](https://go.dev/ref/spec#Handling_panics) distinguishes abrupt propagation from ordinary return; [Rust try propagation](https://doc.rust-lang.org/reference/expressions/operator-expr.html#the-try-propagation-expression) returns typed outcomes; [LLVM regression guidance](https://llvm.org/docs/TestingGuide.html#regression-tests) favors minimal reproductions. These are analogies, not Schway semantic authorities or dependencies. [WG14 N1570](https://www.open-std.org/jtc1/sc22/wg14/www/docs/n1570.pdf) ``6.4.4.4, 7.21.7.4 and 7.21.8.2 directly support the C escape/NUL/counted-write distinction. The C17 draft and Open Group pages could not be fetched; no claim relies on their unseen contents.
