# Phase 37.1 prohibition lifecycle closeout

This is a scoped closeout amendment for the four plan prohibitions owned by
Phase 37.1. It resolves the test-tier lifecycle after the actual-source machine
proofs passed. It does not mark Phase 37.1 complete or replace the downstream
phase review, security, validation, independent verification, freshness, or
M007 audit gates.

## Authority and limits

This lifecycle resolution follows the delegated normal GSD test-tier path:
semantic negative controls, clean/default controls, immutable-source checks,
and fresh canonical producers. No human-judgment tier or human approval is
claimed. The transition is limited to these four existing prohibitions and
their current evidence bindings. Hosted/Linux receipts, physical cleanup,
numeric-peer guarantees, and the remaining phase gates are outside this
closeout.
