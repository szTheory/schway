<assumption_delta_decision>
**Primary noun:** *a `data` alternative, which may or may not carry a payload.*
Phase 12 ends the monopoly of the payload-less alternative.

**Decision at the alternative level: `promote`.** `core.DataType.Alternatives
[]string` stays as the byte-frozen name list (D-12-08), but the per-alternative
detail record added in plan 02 becomes the primary description of what an
alternative *is*, with "nullary" expressed as the zero-payload case of that one
record rather than as a separate kind of alternative. The single constructor
function (D-12-09) is the mechanism that makes the promoted form the only
constructible one.

**Decision at the `OperationKind` level: `add-alongside` — accepted debt.**
D-12-05 locks two new kinds beside the existing ten rather than generalizing
"operation with a payload slot" into a first-class shape. This is deliberate:
the whole point of D-12-05 is that a new kind is what forces
`TestAllOperationKindsHandledAtEverySite` to demand real coverage, which a
generalized shape would not.

*What would force a later promote:* a third payload-shaped kind arriving — most
likely multi-field payloads, or resource-carrying payloads landing under
D-12-30's three-part condition. At three kinds the per-kind arm duplication
across six dispatch sites stops paying for itself and the promotion becomes
correct.

*Suggested (not required) contract test:* a test asserting that every
`OperationKind` naming a payload slot populates `PayloadTargetID`, so a future
kind cannot reintroduce the "payload hides in a conditionally-populated field on
a general-purpose kind" shape D-12-05 rejected.
</assumption_delta_decision>
