<objective>
Make NAT-05 true in the only honest way available under D-11-09 — an explicit,
visible, generated empty set — and then stand the mid-phase gate up as a
four-part conjunction with a genuine second knower, prove it non-vacuous by
mutation, and adjudicate it in writing.

Purpose: the gate separates "new infrastructure works" from "optimizer promises
are sound", so a failure in the second cannot be masked by a defect in the first.
Per D-11-09 the zero-attribute state is terminal for this phase, which makes the
gate's non-vacuity the whole load-bearing claim: the corpus had real aliasing to
promise about, we emitted zero promises, and it still compiled, linked, ran and
agreed.

Output: the empty-set emission and its test, the gate lane and its mutation kill,
and `11-MIDPHASE-GATE.md`'s written verdict.
</objective>
