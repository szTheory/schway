<objective>
Make `interp` STABLE, not merely working — the roadmap's own hard precondition
for Phase 11 — by freezing its emitted schema and shape against a golden corpus,
proving its determinism, and replacing the absent coverage story with an
enumerated structural floor.

phase, for the component that roughly doubles in size here and becomes the
authority everything native is differential-tested against. A stability claim
backed by a paragraph is not a precondition; a stability claim backed by a
byte-for-byte golden corpus and an enumerated coverage table is.

Ordering: this plan runs LAST, after SEM-09 (plan 10-05). That ordering is
non-negotiable — the freeze is broken inside Phase 10 itself if declared before
the drop-ordering event emission is final. It is the phase's top review risk.

Output: the golden corpus and its drift test, the documented regeneration and
escalation procedures, the determinism check, and the coverage-floor table in
`10-VALIDATION.md`.
</objective>
