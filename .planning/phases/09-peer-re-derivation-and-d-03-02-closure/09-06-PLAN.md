## Planner findings the executor must not re-derive

- **The peer already counts its own work.** `corevalidate.Result.Checks`
  (`corevalidate.go:59`, populated at `:111` from `v.checks`) is an exported,
  deterministic counted-work value incremented at named sites throughout the
  validator — including `foldChain`'s "one unit per NEWLY visited place"
  (`:1192`) and `recomputeLoanEndpoints`' per-operation and per-loan units. No new
  counter infrastructure is needed; the instrument exists.
- **The peer's shape is already non-iterative and already documented as such.**
  `recomputeLoanEndpoints` (`:1270-1403`) is a one-shot reachability closure —
  `blockReach` (`:1215`) is BFS-once-per-block with a visited-once frontier — plus
  a static reduction over a fully-materialized use relation. Never a worklist,
  never iterated to convergence. That is precisely why its curve differs from
  `check`'s and needs its own bound.
- **`check`'s disclosure seam already exists and is already proven closed.**
  `interproceduralConsultObserved` (`check.go:641`, fired at `:599-606`) plus
  `TestInterproceduralDisclosedFieldSet` (`check_test.go:4898`) establish that
  `check`'s consulted set is exactly `{"return.mode", "parameters[0].mode"}`,
  corpus-wide, as a compile-time constant. The peer mirrors the pattern; it does
  not re-invent it.
- **`export_test.go` symbols are visible only inside `corevalidate`'s own test
  binary.** They are NOT reachable from package `check`. That is why the peer's
  consulted set is surfaced through an exported `Result` accessor rather than a
  test-only setter — following the exact reason `PeerSignatures()` and
  `PeerSiteCoverage()` are exported today.
- **Both naming chokepoints were widened for this metric in plan 09-02.** If the
  new metric name is missing from either, stop: the bound would be silently
  demoted to `observed` and could never block (T-09-04).
