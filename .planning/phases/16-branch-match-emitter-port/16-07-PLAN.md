## D-16-09 execution amendment

under the deliberately retained dispatch architecture: legacy N=1 output is
`lang.execution/1`, while direct `emitProgram` is `lang.execution/2`. The
measured difference is intentional and already recorded by the retained
characterization; forcing equality would require Plan 16-09's forbidden early
dispatch/schema cutover. Per the user's autonomous continuation direction,
this plan records and guards that provenance rather than asserting a false
identity. Plan 16-09 owns the final byte-identity gate: after the atomic flip,
both public APIs must be byte-identical to their corresponding direct
`emitProgram` invocation on every admitted row. This preserves the semantic
four-tier gate and adds no admission for cut M004 families.
