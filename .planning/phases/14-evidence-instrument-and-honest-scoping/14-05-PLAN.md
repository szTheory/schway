<objective>
Make the project's two self-describing artefacts machine-checked and currently
true: `LANGUAGE-MATURITY.md`'s counts, and the budget manifest's suite wall-clock
baseline.

Purpose: `LANGUAGE-MATURITY.md` is the file every planner is told to read before
re-deriving what the language can express, and it is wrong — it states a guard
count that no longer matches the tree, and its corpus figures have drifted in
both directions. The budget manifest is an input to this project's own
feedback-budget reasoning, and the wall-clock figure the documents carry is off by
roughly three-fold. Both are stale self-description inside a phase about
instruments that must execute their claims.

Output: a self-check test that re-derives every stated count independently, a
corrected maturity document, and an observed wall-clock manifest row measured
cold during this phase.
</objective>
