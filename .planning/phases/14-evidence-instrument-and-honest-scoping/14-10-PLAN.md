<objective>
Close the loop: reconcile every runnability, groundedness and grep finding the
lint reports, outside the archives it found them in, under a closed verdict
vocabulary where every verdict carries an obligation the lint itself checks; ship
`.planning/EVIDENCE-RECONCILIATION.md` as a generated byte-compared view; and
drive those three classes of the pinned frontier to empty.

Purpose: this is the archive crux. Detection is in and editing is out. A dead row
is **never rewritten in place** — that would be exactly the falsification this
phase exists to prevent — and excluding the archives entirely would make the lint
find nothing and be theater. The correction therefore lives outside the archive,
keyed to the original command, and this is explicitly **not a baseline file**: a
baseline is satisfied by silence, while every entry here is satisfied only by a
claim that can itself fail.

Output: the reconciliation entries as witnessed debt rows, the generated view with
its fail-closed anti-decay guards and its commit-coupling rule, and an emptied
frontier for three of four classes.
</objective>

<threat_model>
## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| archived document → reconciliation entry | The archive is read-only evidence; the correction lives outside it and must not be able to alter it |
| reconciliation entry → generated view | The entry is authored truth; the view is derived and must never become a second source |
| commit contents → coupling rule | A commit is the unit at which an archive edit becomes visible or invisible |

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |

| T-14-58 | Tampering | archive rewriting | high | mitigate | Corrections live outside the archive keyed to the original command; the archived tree is asserted unmodified (Task 1) |
| T-14-59 | Repudiation | laundering a dead pattern | high | mitigate | The rename verdict's replacement command must itself resolve, with a passing negative subtest (Task 1) |
| T-14-60 | Spoofing | obsolete-by-design as a rename in disguise | high | mitigate | The verdict asserts the named symbol is absent from the tree, a falsifiable positive claim rather than an empty search (Task 1) |
