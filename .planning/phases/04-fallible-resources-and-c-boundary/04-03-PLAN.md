<objective>
Make the foreign contract inspectable in three layers derived from one authoritative source, and prove the layout claim with the C compiler itself rather than with a bespoke inspector — while emitting nothing the optimizer can act on.

Purpose: ROADMAP success criterion 2 requires that generated C declarations and adapters make target layout, allocator identity, alias and capture, callback retention, and unwind policy inspectable. FFI-01 is the requirement; D-04-10 through D-04-14 are the shape.
Output: the complete `core.ForeignContract`, the generated `_LANG_` header, the generated conformance translation unit compiled separately and not linked, the `lang.foreign/0` sidecar manifest digest-bound into evidence, the layout mutation control against the frozen fixture, and the zero-attribute control against the emitter.
</objective>
