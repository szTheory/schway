<objective>
Give the phase a real, reachable, deliberately terminal defect, and make the evidence for it survive the process dying: events stream as they occur, the terminal record is the last write, and its absence fails the run.

Purpose: ROADMAP success criterion 3's first half is about panic, and "there is no unwind path" is worthless evidence about panic if panic does not exist. Adding the defect terminator costs one operation kind, depends on none of the other decisions, and converts the claim into one with a reachable witness. The buffered emitter would otherwise make an aborting process emit zero events, leaving success criterion 4 unfalsifiable on exactly the paths criterion 3 is about.
Output: the `OpDefect` terminator with an abort-only `_Noreturn` lowering, the closed terminal-outcome axis with `cancelled` reserved and unconstructible, the additive streaming emitter, signal-aware process adjudication, and the no-release-on-defect control.
</objective>
