<assumption_delta_decision>
**Detected:** `pluralization` — "A Lang function can call **another** Lang
function." Phase 07 is where *"every Lang program is a single function"* stops
being true.

**Noun that is now primary:** a `core.Program` carrying **N functions**, with a
**call graph** over them. The single-function program is one degenerate point in
that space (N = 1, zero edges), not the shape the system is built around.

**Decision: `promote`.** The generalized representation is promoted and the old
specific one is demoted to a detail of one variant:
- `FunctionSignature.Parameters` is a **slice** even though the checker admits
  exactly arity 1 (D-07-10) — schema capacity is generalized now, with one
  producer; the arity rule stays tight in the checker where the cost lives.
- `callgraph` roots are **all declared functions**, never "the entry point"
  (D-07-18) — the singular-entry assumption is not encoded anywhere.
- `cgen`'s `len(Functions) != 1` hard fail is the one place the singular
  assumption is deliberately **retained and quarantined** as Phase 11's problem
  (A-02), and it is named in `PHASE-07-DEBT.md` as a declared limitation rather
  than left implicit.

**Rationale:** adding the plural shape alongside the singular one would leave two
identity models — exactly the drift `cgen`'s guard, `FunctionSignature.Parameter`
(singular), and `core_test.go`'s single-function `linearProbeInput` helper
already demonstrate. Promoting now costs one producer; promoting later costs five
consumers.

**Suggested (not required) invariant test:** a test in
`internal/compiler/core/core_test.go` that constructs a two-function
`core.Program` and asserts every non-`cgen` dispatch site completes — so a future
phase that reintroduces a singular-function assumption outside `cgen` goes red
rather than silently narrowing the space.
</assumption_delta_decision>
