<objective>
Stage 1. Make `core.OpCall` real: relax the parser's blanket refusal of a bare
call in a binding RHS, register `OpCall` in `AllOperationKinds()` — deliberately
breaking all six exhaustive switches at once — land the `check` producer path,
and bring both exhaustive-dispatch controls green with their own phase-07 fixture
lists.

Purpose: the registry edit is a forcing function. It converts *"six sites, easy
to forget one"* into *"six failures, impossible to forget one."*

Output: a two-function `.lang` program that `lang check` admits, with `OpCall`
handled at all six sites and both controls green.

**Call-surface decisions this plan implements and honors:**
- **D-07-01** — a call is a binding RHS with **exactly one argument**, and that
  argument is the name of an in-scope binding (`let r = f(x)`).
- **D-07-02** — the ownership convention is read implicitly from the callee's
  declared signature, never written at the call site.
- **D-07-07** — arity-N is **deliberately rejected** this phase; it would force
  multi-argument loan-interaction rules into the phase that first makes `OpCall`
  real. Do not widen arity to make a fixture easier to write.
- **D-07-06** — M001's D-02 (labeled call-site arguments) is **deferred, not
  revoked**; at arity 1 labels carry zero disambiguation. Arguments stay
  positional. It is reinstated when arity widens past 1.
- **D-07-03** — divergent cross-call loan state is expressible today with no new
  grammar (`let v = borrow buffer` then `f(v)`), which is the shape Phase 08's
  mandatory gate needs. No grammar work is owed to Phase 08 here.
- **D-07-05** — for downstream wording: Phase 08's gate says **"call-graph depth
  ≥2"**, not "composition depth". These are A-normal-form calls; composition depth
  comes from call-graph depth, not expression nesting.
</objective>
